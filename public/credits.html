Game presentation

Creature sprites, scenery, item icons and effects are drawn by the project's procedural code. Creature sounds are synthesized with Web Audio. No third-party game images, music or recordings are included in the current source tree.

Speech verification uses a tester-supplied recording, retained only in that page's memory. No speech-test recording is distributed.
